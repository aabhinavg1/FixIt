## What this flag actually does

Controls whether the array temporaries created under **-frepack-arrays** are allocated on the stack or on the heap. By default, the heap is used. Allocations of polymorphic types are always done on the heap, though this may change in future releases.



It can be negated with -fstack-repack-arrays.
